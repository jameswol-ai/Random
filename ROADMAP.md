RANDOM

Architecture Intelligence Operating System (AIOS)

«Vision: Build an autonomous AI platform that transforms human ideas into intelligent, construction-ready buildings.»

---

Mission

RANDOM is an open, modular Architecture Intelligence Operating System that combines artificial intelligence, engineering, Building Information Modeling (BIM), and evolutionary optimization to assist architects, engineers, planners, and construction professionals throughout the entire building lifecycle.

The goal is not simply to generate floor plans, but to reason about design decisions, evaluate engineering constraints, estimate costs, verify compliance, and continuously improve through learning.

---

Core Principles

- Modular architecture
- Agent-based intelligence
- Explainable AI decisions
- Continuous learning
- Engineering-first design
- Open plugin ecosystem
- BIM-native workflows
- Scalable and maintainable code

Phase 9: Autonomous AIOS

Natural-language building design.

Example:

«"Design a sustainable four-bedroom home on a 25 × 40 m site with a budget of $150,000."»

The system should automatically:

1. Understand the request.
2. Generate design alternatives.
3. Optimize layouts.
4. Perform engineering analysis.
5. Estimate quantities and costs.
6. Check compliance.
7. Build a BIM model.
8. Produce drawings.
9. Generate reports.
10. Export construction-ready documentation.
